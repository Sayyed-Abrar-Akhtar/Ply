# Ply Design

Ply is contemporary apparel for menswear, womenswear and unisex wardrobes. The visual direction is quiet, tactile and editorial rather than fast-fashion or SaaS-like.

## Tokens
Ink #171715 for garment-like depth; Bone #F5F3EE for a soft studio ground; Stone #D9D5CC for material surfaces; Charcoal #5C5A54 for supporting copy; Cobalt #3157D5 is the only accent and is reserved for action, sale and wishlist states.

Inter Tight is the display face; Inter is used for body and UI. Spacing follows an 8px rhythm. Sections use one shared vertical padding token and all content uses one max-width container.

## Layout
Home: full-bleed editorial hero, product grid, asymmetric brand story, newsletter. Collection: heading, filters, sort, product grid. Product: large gallery beside a purchase panel. Utility pages stay narrow and readable.

## Motion
One deliberate hero reveal; otherwise motion is action-based only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Principles
Fabric, silhouette and useful product information carry the experience. Chrome stays restrained so the site feels like a wardrobe rather than a catalogue template.
